Activate your GCI ParentVUE account

ParentVUE helps you stay up to date on your student’s grades and attendance at GCI. Checking it regularly is a helpful way to follow their progress, talk with them about how things are going, and reach out to an instructor when questions come up. GCI uses a separate ParentVUE login from your child’s home school district.

Open GCI ParentVUE

Already have a GCI account? Sign in with your GCI credentials or use “Forgot Password” on the login page.

First-time setup:

  1. Request an activation key from GCI@geneseeisd.org or the GCI main office at 810-591-4462.
  2. Open GCI ParentVUE and choose More Options → Activate Account.
  3. Enter the name and activation key provided by the school, then create your username and password.

Snack donations support financial literacy

If your family is able, please consider donating individually packaged, shelf-stable snacks for our classroom store.

We use ClassBank as a digital classroom economy. Students earn virtual classroom dollars through jobs and positive contributions, then practice budgeting, saving, and spending.

Donated snacks stock our classroom store. Students can purchase them with classroom dollars, connecting financial literacy with their effort and responsibility in class.

Students can bring donations to class. Contributions are optional, and any amount is appreciated. Thank you for helping make these lessons meaningful!

Student Helpdesk and computer donations

Our Student Helpdesk gives students hands-on experience troubleshooting computer problems and making repairs. If you have an out-of-warranty computer or device that needs attention, we’re happy to take a look!

We also accept donations of old computers for students to troubleshoot, repair, and learn from. These projects help students build practical IT support skills while helping our community.

Please email Dominic Cascarelli or Andrew Spiece to discuss a device or arrange a donation.

3D Printing

Our student-run print farm now has 12 printers and can handle larger orders as well as individual items. Students gain hands-on experience managing orders, operating printers, and producing finished prints.

Browse MakerWorld for 3D models to find something you’d like printed. We currently accept ready-to-print files only; custom design orders are not available.

Current pricing:

  • Printer time: $1.50 per hour.
  • Filament: $20 per kilogram of material used, in addition to printer time.
  • Tax: Applicable sales tax is added to the order total.
  • Larger orders: Discounts are available.

Example: A print using two hours of printer time and 100 grams of filament costs $5 before tax and any discount ($3 for printer time + $2 for filament).

Please encourage your student to use the Student Print Submission form and choose Personal Project for a family order. They can submit a ready-to-print file or MakerWorld link with the order. The form also lists “Idea / design help,” but that service is not currently available for family orders.

Submit a print order

The form’s cost estimator provides an estimate, not a confirmed quote. Our confirmed-quote system is not yet available; the rates above apply, with sales tax added and discounts available for larger orders.

Every order supports our students. Proceeds from print orders help cover competition travel, apparel, and gear for competitions and Work Based Learning. Our goal is to cover program-related costs so every student can participate regardless of their family’s ability to pay.
