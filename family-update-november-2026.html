Activate your GCI ParentVUE account

ParentVUE lets you check GCI attendance, progress reports, and report cards. GCI uses a separate ParentVUE login from your child’s home school district.

  1. Request an activation key from GCI@geneseeisd.org or the GCI main office at 810-591-4462.
  2. Open GCI ParentVUE and choose More Options → Activate Account.
  3. Enter the name and activation key provided by the school, then create your username and password.

Open GCI ParentVUE

Already have a GCI account? Sign in with your GCI credentials or use “Forgot Password” on the login page.
