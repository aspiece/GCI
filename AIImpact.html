Data sources

These studies and tools provide the reference values used in this calculator.

Microsoft Research — Energy Use of AI Inference Supports the 0.34 Wh typical text-query estimate and the 4.32 Wh extended-reasoning estimate. Luccioni, Jernite & Strubell — Power Hungry Processing Provides the image-generation benchmark used for the image preset. Berkeley Lab — U.S. Data Center Energy Usage Report Supports the national-average estimate of approximately 0.36 L/kWh of direct on-site cooling water. Berkeley Lab — Water IMPACT Tool Supports the 4.52 L/kWh electricity-related water reference used in the total water footprint.

The calculator combines estimates from sources with different methods and system boundaries. Its results are intended for classroom comparison, not as exact measurements of a specific AI service.

Actual impact varies by model, response length, hardware, location, cooling system, and electricity source. The rewrite, summary, and document-analysis presets use simple classroom multipliers based on the typical text-query estimate.
