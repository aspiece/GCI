About the calendar data and coverage

The viewer checks the published master calendar and uses a dated copy from November 23, 2026 when live updates are unavailable. It does not report emergency closures in real time. Official school and district announcements take precedence.

Categories help group different wording into a consistent view. The original event wording and any available source or verification date appear when you select a school. A partial closure affecting only certain grades or sessions may still appear under No School; always read the individual details.

The availability of dates depends on what is included in the source spreadsheet. Check official school calendars for any missing or unconfirmed dates.
