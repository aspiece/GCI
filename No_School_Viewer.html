About the calendar data and coverage

The viewer reads a published master calendar spreadsheet. It shows reported school dates, not real-time emergency closure announcements. School and district notices take precedence, and some building-specific schedules may differ.

The availability of dates depends on what is included in the source spreadsheet. Check official school calendars for any missing or unconfirmed dates.
