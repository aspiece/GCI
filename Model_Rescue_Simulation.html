Mission Briefing

Learn what happened, what your role is, and how you will look closely at the data, test, improve, and decide.

Human review stays in charge, even if the model improves.

What happened

A made-up (fictional) company stopped using an AI model. The model kept making the same mistake. You are the human reviewer. You will look closely at its training data, test its answers, add better examples, and decide what should happen next.

Your four-part process

  1. Look: Find patterns in the training data.
  2. Test: Compare your labels with the model's labels.
  3. Improve: Add examples that fill the missing area.
  4. Decide: Choose whether the model needs more testing or more changes.
Reminder: This simulation uses made-up data. A model's answer is information to check, not proof. A trained person must make the final decision.
Key terms
Training data
Examples used to teach a model.
Label
The correct category for a record.
Accuracy
The percentage of answers the model gets correct.
Human review
A person checks the model's work.
Model version
A model before or after its training data changes.
