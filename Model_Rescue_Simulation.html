Your Mission: Rescue the Model

A fictional company stopped using its AI system because it keeps making the same mistake. Your job is to look at the training data, find the problem, fix it, and decide if the model is ready for more testing.

  • Follow the same data story from Version 1 through Version 2.
  • Look for patterns across records, not just one surprising case.
  • Keep human review in charge even if the model improves.
Learn more about this mission

Success Criteria

  • Pick a career setting and use the scores to understand what the data means.
  • Test Version 1 and find the pattern of errors — don't just look at one case.
  • Pick three new training records that fix the specific weakness you found.
  • Decide if the improved model should only move forward with a qualified person reviewing it.

Warning: Fictional data only

Everything in this simulation is fake and for learning only. Do not type in real names, real workplaces, or real patient information.

  • Use the built-in records only.
  • Model output is a clue, not proof.
  • A trained professional must review all evidence before any real decision is made.

Quick glossary

Open the glossary for key terms

Training data: the examples the model studies to learn how to make predictions.

Feature: one score the model looks at when judging a record.

Label: the correct answer attached to a record.

Borderline case: a record with middle scores that is hard to label correctly when the training data is missing similar examples.
